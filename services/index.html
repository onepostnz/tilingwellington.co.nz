How Long Does A Typical Tiling Project Take?+

Tiling projects can be a great way to upgrade the look of your home or business. But how long does it take to finish such a project? Well, Wellington Tiling Pros have got you covered!

The time required for tiling depends on several factors:
1) The size of the area that needs to be done;
2) The type and complexity of the tile pattern;
3) Any repairs or prep work needed before installation.

On average, most tiling jobs will require at least two days for completion. However, if there are any unforeseen issues like cracks in walls or floors that need repair, then it could easily take up to three days or more. In addition, larger areas with intricate patterns may mean more labor is necessary and thus extend the duration of the job even further. With our experienced team of professionals at Wellington Tiling Pros, however, we guarantee quality results within an agreed-upon timeframe.

What Type Of Maintenance Do Tiled Surfaces Require?+

When it comes to tiling, maintenance is an important aspect of keeping surfaces looking great and lasting longer. Wellington Tiling Pros understand this, which is why we take care to provide the necessary information on how best to maintain your tiles.

We always recommend regularly cleaning any dirt off your tiled surfaces with a damp cloth or mop. Doing so will reduce the chances of grout discolouration or mould growth over time. Additionally, you should:
• Clean up spills immediately using warm water and mild soap
\t- Lightly scrub with soft bristled brush for tougher stains
\t- Rinse thoroughly and completely dry after each use
• Use protective mats in areas that get heavy foot traffic such as entryways or kitchens
\t- Mats made from rubber are highly recommended
\t- Change them often for maximum protection
• Regrouting when needed to keep dirt out of gaps between tiles
\t- Consider professional regrouting if necessary
\t- Sealant can be used afterwards for extra protection

The key takeaway here is that taking proper care and preventive measures are essential when it comes to maintaining tiled surfaces. With some basic effort put into regular cleaning and occasional deep cleans, you can help ensure your tiles look beautiful and last longer.
